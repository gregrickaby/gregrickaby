Hello there! I'm a [software engineer](/resume), [self-taught photographer](/photos), and author of [_Creating a Website for Dummies Jr._](https://amzn.to/41eZFfF) (Wiley Global, 2017), based in Alabama.

I've been building websites since 1997. First came [Geocities](/geocities) pages, then my first real job: radio station websites running on a custom CMS written in classic ASP. WordPress took over in 2006 and kept me busy for 17 years. React showed up in 2019, and in 2023 I moved to Next.js for good.

These days I build with AI tools. Nearly 30 years on the web has taught me how to adapt to ever-changing tech. And when a teammate gets stuck, I'm often the one they ask (my favorite part of the job, honestly).

When I'm not at a keyboard or looking through a camera, there's a good chance I'm reading a book, cheering for my kids at their sporting events, or planning our next trip to Walt Disney World, where we've been Annual Passholders and Disney Vacation Club members for years.
